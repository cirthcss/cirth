Why the form was the product

What a checkout teaches about semantic HTML

By Grace Hopper · 21 September 2026 · 6 min read

The first version of the checkout had forty components and one <form>. The second had one form, and the browser did the rest: validation, autofill and the keyboard came for free the moment each field said what it was.

The most expensive widget is the one that already exists.

A lesson learned twice

Three rules we kept

  1. Every field has a label you can see.
  2. Errors are words next to the field, not colours.
  3. Submitting works with Enter, because it is a form.
